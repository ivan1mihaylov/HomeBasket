**A product no longer needs a barcode.**

Bread from the bakery, tomatoes, a line someone typed on a shopping list — none
of it has a barcode you ever scan, and until now none of it could be a product
here. Such a product is kept under a key of its own instead, and shows in the
card as **No barcode**.

They arrive on their own from HomeBasket Lists 0.13.0: whatever is configured
on a list — name, picture, the kind of shop it comes from — is handed over, so
it is offered by name everywhere else, with all of it.

A product's sheet has a field to **attach a barcode by hand**. From then on the
barcode belongs to that product, and scanning it finds the name, the picture
and the shop already set rather than making a second one.

Needs HomeBasket Card 0.11.0, which is where a barcode is typed in.
